## Slide 3 · 主线一：模型读取与信息处理

**English**

> Let me start with the model side. First, the door–room link. The old problem: some rooms had no solid geometry, so we could not tell which room a door belonged to. Our fix: give every room a floor footprint from its bounding box, include the rotation in the placement matrix, and prefer real boundary data when it exists. The result: escape distance can now be judged for 98 rooms, and we found two real violations — two rooms that have no exit door at all.
>
> Second, fire-hydrant spacing. We taught the extractor to read fire-pipe geometry, take the centre point of each pipe, and compute the distance to its nearest neighbour. This moves us from reading attributes to reading geometry and computing distances. On the office sample, six fire pipes are 13 metres apart — well under the 30-metre limit.

**中文提示**：门-房间关联方法（bbox 足迹 + 放置矩阵旋转 + 边界优先）→ 98 房可判、查出 2 处真违规；消火栓间距方法（消防管质心最近邻）→ 从读属性升级到读几何算距离，office 6 根管 13m ≤ 30m。

## Slide 4 · 主线二：规则扩量

**English**

> Why can we now match the code? Because our rules hold real thresholds taken from the standards: GB 55037, GB 50016, GB 50974, and the Singapore Fire Code. Let me explain the principle — three points. One: use real fields only. A rule only uses fields the extractor really outputs, so we have no dead rules. Two: scope by condition. A rule judges only matching elements — for example, only fire pipes. Three: cite the code. Every rule points to the exact clause, and the threshold is verified from the source text — a door must be 0.9 metres wide, hydrants 30 metres apart.
>
> The effect: a question finds a clause, the clause maps to a rule, and the rule makes a real judgement on the model. The knowledge base has moved from search to a basis for judgement, and the rule set grew from 21 to 47 rules per region.

**中文提示**：规则扩量的原理三要素：①真实字段（对齐提取器、无死规则）②条件限定范围（如只判 fire 管）③规范出处（code_ref 对应条款、阈值按原文核实 0.9m/30m）。作用：提问→条文→规则→真判定；21→47 条/地区。

## Slide 5 · 主线三：B-Rep 调研

**English**

> We surveyed four sample models and counted the B-Rep solids. Three of them carry B-Rep by default — Schependomlaan has more than four thousand. This changed our plan. Originally we planned to use CSG plus Boolean operations. But CSG keeps the modelling history and needs a self-built solid kernel, which is expensive. Let me make the difference clear: CSG is a parametric construction that keeps history; B-Rep is an explicit shell — surfaces that close into a solid. Since B-Rep is already there, we use it directly. The divergence theorem, with signed tetrahedra, gives us the exact volume — we verified it on columns, beams and slabs. So Step 3 is a B-Rep / mesh mix with a precision note. We do not need a CSG kernel.

**中文提示**：调研计数（Schep 4202 / office 362 / FZK 140 / Duplex 0）→ B-Rep 主流自带；原本计划 CSG+布尔、麻烦在自研内核；CSG=参数化保留历史，B-Rep=显式外壳；散度定理（有符号四面体）算体积已验证 → 台阶③ 走 B-Rep/网格混合，不用 CSG。

## Demo · 现场演示讲稿（跑 demo 用，约 2 分钟）

**开场 · 先讲清楚这个 py 的来由**

> Why did we write this small script? Our platform UI shows the final review report, but not the inside of a judgement — which clause was hit, which rule it matched, and why. So we made a small command-line demo (`scripts/demo_reasoning.py`) that prints the whole chain: **question → found clause → matched rule → judgement**. These are its outputs on two models.

中文提示：先说明——平台只给最终报告、看不到判断过程，所以做了命令行 demo 把链路打出来（提问→条文→规则→判定），下面是运行结果。

**屏幕 A · 公寓（CN · Schependomlaan）—— 完整链路 + 诚实上报**

> First, the apartment model. Door width found **11 real violations**: door 2.01 is 0.795 m, below the 0.9 m minimum. Now the honest part — the model has no electrical equipment, so emergency lighting reports **"not checked"**; and it has 60 pipes but no fire pipes, so hydrant spacing reports **"no condition match"**. It never fakes a pass.

屏幕旁边英文注解：
> Apartment — Full Chain, Honest Reporting
> - Door width: 11 real violations — door 2.01 is 0.795 m, below the 0.9 m minimum
> - No electrical equipment → emergency lighting reports "not checked"
> - 60 pipes but no fire pipes → hydrant spacing reports "no condition match"

中文提示：公寓屏——门宽 11 处违规（2.01 门 0.795m<0.9m）+ 两处"未检查"（无电气 / 有管无 fire 管），它不假装通过。

**屏幕 B · 办公楼（CN · office_3f）—— 数据补齐，规则激活**

> Same questions, a data-complete office model. Emergency lighting finds **3 real violations** — corridors without emergency lights. Fire hydrants: **6 pipes, spacing 13.0 m ≤ 30 m → all pass**. The point: better model reading wakes the rules up automatically.

屏幕旁边英文注解：
> Office — Same Questions, Data Complete
> - Emergency lighting: 3 real violations (corridor without emergency lights)
> - Fire hydrants: 6 pipes, spacing 13.0 m ≤ 30 m → all pass

中文提示：办公楼屏——应急照明 3 处真违规、消火栓 6 根 13m≤30m 全过 → 数据补齐规则就自动激活。

**过渡句（放两屏中间或收尾）**

> Better model reading → rules wake up automatically. No data = honest "not checked"; full data = real judgement.

中文提示：模型读取越好，规则越活；没数据就诚实"未检查"，有数据就真判定。
